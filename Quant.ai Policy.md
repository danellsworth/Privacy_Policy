# Privacy Policy for Quant.ai App

**Last Updated:** February 22, 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the Quant.ai software application ("Application") created by Dan Ellsworth. The Application is a stock research and analysis app available on the Apple App Store and Google Play.

## What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

### User Provided Information

The Application does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as name, email, or phone number.

### Subscription and Purchase Information

If you purchase a subscription (Quant.ai Pro), your payment is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment details such as credit card numbers. Apple and Google may share limited purchase information with us, such as whether you have an active subscription and your subscription type, solely for the purpose of providing you with the correct features.

### Automatically Collected Information

The Application itself does not directly collect personal data such as your IP address, device identifiers, or usage patterns.

However, the Application uses third-party services that may automatically collect certain information:

- **Advertising:** The free version of the Application displays ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information about your device and ad interactions, including device identifiers, IP address, and browsing behavior, in order to serve relevant advertisements. This data collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Analytics:** The Application may use third-party analytics services to help us understand usage patterns and improve the Application. These services may collect anonymized usage data such as app opens, feature usage, and crash reports.

Quant.ai Pro subscribers who have ad-free access are not subject to data collection by third-party ad networks.

## Does the Application collect precise real-time location information of the device?

The Application does not collect precise information about the location of your mobile device.

## Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

Third-party ad networks and analytics providers may collect information as described above. We do not sell, share, or transfer your personal information to third parties for purposes unrelated to the operation of the Application.

## What are my opt-out rights?

- **Ads and Ad Tracking:** You can limit ad tracking through your device settings. On iOS, go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, go to Settings > Privacy > Ads. You may also upgrade to Quant.ai Pro to remove all third-party advertising.
- **Analytics:** You may disable analytics collection through your device's privacy settings where applicable.

## Data Retention Policy, Managing Your Information

The Application stores user settings, favorites, and preferences locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to or stored on our servers. If you delete the Application, this locally stored data will be removed.

Subscription status information retained by Apple or Google is governed by their respective data retention policies.

## Children

The Application is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly solicit data from or market to children under the age of 13.

## Security

We are concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. Since the Application primarily stores data locally on your device, the security of that data is largely dependent on the security of your device. Please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage, is 100% secure.

## Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time for any reason. We will notify you of any changes to our Privacy Policy by posting the new Privacy Policy here. You are advised to consult this Privacy Policy regularly for any changes. Continued use of the Application after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Your Consent

By using the Application, you are consenting to the collection and use of information as set forth in this Privacy Policy.
